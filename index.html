investe is a private investment business. We acquire, hold and improve real estate, and take considered positions in a small number of private ventures. We invest our own capital, on our own timeline.

01

Approach

  1. Long horizons

    We buy to hold. We measure decisions in decades, not cycles, and we’re comfortable waiting for the right asset.

  2. Considered acquisitions

    We assess every opportunity on fundamentals: location, land, income and the downside. We make fewer decisions, and better ones.

  3. Hands-on stewardship

    We stay close to what we own. We maintain it, improve it and add value over time.

  4. Discretion

    We work quietly, deal directly with the other party, and keep our own counsel.
